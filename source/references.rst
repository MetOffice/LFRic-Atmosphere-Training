.. Conventions for adding a reference:

   * Cite from any page with ``[Label]_``. Where the prose names the authors,
     keep the names and follow them with the label, e.g.
     ``Kuhlbrodt et al. (2018) [Kuhlbrodt_et_al_2018]_``.
   * Labels are ``Surname_Year``, ``Surname_and_Surname_Year`` or
     ``Surname_et_al_Year`` (three or more authors), ASCII letters only.
   * Entries follow the American Meteorological Society (AMS) style, link to
     the DOI where one exists, and are kept in alphabetical order.
